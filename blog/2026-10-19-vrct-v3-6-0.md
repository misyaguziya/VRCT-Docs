VRCT **v3.6.0** adds two new ways to use VRCT: **Chat Transcribe**, which reads VRChat chat bubbles on screen, and the **VR UI (Beta)**, which lets you operate VRCT from inside VR. It also brings VAD-based voice detection, a redesigned updater, and security improvements.

<!-- truncate -->

## Highlights

### Chat Transcribe (OCR)
- Reads VRChat chat bubbles on screen and translates them through the usual translation pipeline.
- Results appear in the message log and the SteamVR overlay. They are never sent to the VRChat chatbox.
- Choose the source language, the capture window name, the capture interval, and the recognition thresholds in the new **Chat Transcribe** tab.

### VR UI (Beta)
- A launcher on your wrist turns Translation, Microphone, Listen, and Chat Transcribe on and off.
- Floating windows show the log, the language selection, and the VR settings. They can be moved, resized, anchored, and locked.
- Translation and transcription pause while the VR settings window is open.

### Voice detection (VAD)
- The mic and speaker sensitivity modes now include **VAD (Auto)**, which uses Silero VAD to send audio to transcription each time you finish speaking.

### Updater
- The updater screen is redesigned. You can choose the release channel (Stable or Beta), the CPU or GPU (CUDA) build, and the version to install, including an earlier release from v3.6.0 onward.
- A comparison table shows what changes before you install.
- The downloaded installer is checked against its SHA-256 checksum before it runs.

### Settings
- Search the settings from the top of the Config Window.
- The translation tab is grouped into AI Translation (Local), External Translation API, and Local LLM & Compatible Server.
- The WebSocket server now requires an access token. Use **WebSocket URL (click to copy)** in the Advanced Settings tab to get the URL with the token.
