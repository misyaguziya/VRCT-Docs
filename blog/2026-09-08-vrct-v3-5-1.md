VRCT **v3.5.1** is a maintenance release focused on transcription recording
and installer reliability.

<!-- truncate -->

## Highlights

### Transcription
- Set **Mic Record Timeout** or **Speaker Record Timeout** to **0** for
  unlimited recording. Phrase boundaries continue to use silence detection
  and the corresponding Phrase Timeout setting.
- This fixes recording being interrupted immediately when a zero timeout was
  selected.

### Installer
- The installer checks available disk space before downloading and extracting
  the selected CPU or GPU package, and reports the required and available space
  when it cannot continue.
- Large package downloads and extraction are more resilient, with parallel
  downloads when appropriate and fresh retries when a download or extraction
  fails.
- The installer requires Windows 10 version 1803 (April 2018) or later.
